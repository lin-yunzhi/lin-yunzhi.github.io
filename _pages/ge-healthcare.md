## Description
During my Mechanical Engineering internship at GE HealthCare, I contributed to the development of neonatal incubators and warmers. I worked across mechanical design, safety and risk analysis, FEA, Design of Experiments (DOE), design verification and validation (V&V), and manufacturing readiness for NPI electromechanical systems.

## Mechanical Design & Manufacturing
I designed 13 production-ready GD&T drawings for sheet metal and injection-molded components in Creo. I performed tolerance stack-up analyses, ANSYS FEA, and DFMA reviews to evaluate component interfaces, structural performance, manufacturability, and assembly fit. This work supported electromechanical integration and manufacturing readiness.

## Safety & Risk Analysis
I assessed suspended-mass safety risks across 90+ components using energy calculations and FMEA to identify potential failure modes and evaluate their consequences. I implemented secondary retention, fail-safe mechanisms, and poka-yoke features to mitigate mechanical safety risks and support compliance with medical device safety requirements.

## Engineering Innovation
I co-authored a defensive publication proposing an AI-integrated workflow for suspended-mass analysis, exploring how AI could improve workflow efficiency while maintaining engineering review and traceability.

## Design Verification & Validation
I supported design verification and validation by designing test fixtures, developing and executing DOEs, and analyzing experimental data. My work included electronics thermal management, center-of-gravity assessment, sensor testing, and acoustic characterization. I used the resulting data to evaluate system performance against design requirements and support compliance with ISO and IEC 60601 requirements.=
